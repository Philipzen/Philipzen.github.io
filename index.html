Something between Sports and Design.

Hi, I’m Marie and I study Communication Design in Mannheim.

My focus is on graphic and motion design. I’m especially interested in the connection between sports, movement and design.

Sport is a big part of my everyday life and therefore flows into my work as well. I like experimenting with different techniques and trying out how movement and dynamics can be shown in new, playful ways.
